What if I need help during the trial? There's a feedback and support form built into the app that pre-fills your version and record counts, so you're not re-explaining your setup. We read and reply to what comes through it.
How do I cancel? Billing runs through Stripe, and you can cancel any time, you're not locked into a term. Cancelling stops future billing, it doesn't touch your records, which stay on your device either way.
