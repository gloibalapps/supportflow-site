# About SupportFlow™

## Built for support workers

SupportFlow™ was created to make the business side of independent support work easier to manage.

Support work is about people, but working as a sole trader also means keeping on top of shifts, participant records, invoices, travel, expenses, tax and paperwork.

Those things often end up spread across different apps, spreadsheets, notes and folders.

SupportFlow brings them together.

## What SupportFlow helps you manage

Depending on the features available in your version, SupportFlow can help you keep track of:

- Participants and support information
- Shifts and timesheets
- Shift notes
- Invoices
- Travel and kilometres
- Business expenses
- Service agreements
- Tax estimates
- Your day-to-day sole trader records

The goal is simple: **less time managing paperwork and more confidence that your business records are organised.**

## Designed around privacy

SupportFlow™ uses a local-first approach.

Your participant and operational records are stored on your device rather than in a central SupportFlow participant database.

That design was intentional.

Participant information can be sensitive, and SupportFlow aims to collect and centralise as little of that information as reasonably possible.

You remain in control of your records, your backups and the information you choose to share.

## Claimable

Claimable is an optional deduction checker built into SupportFlow.


Claimable is separate from your locally stored participant records and is designed not to read or automatically include those records.

Do not enter participant names, NDIS numbers, health information or other personal information into Claimable.
